Own product / computer vision

Deinorus Fall Detector

Watches a care-home room through an ordinary camera and alerts staff when it detects a fall, even if the person gets back up. If they stay down, the alert escalates, and only a staff member can close it. A pose model turns each frame into a skeleton, so the alert engine and the staff panel only receive skeletons, zones and events.

  • Pose estimation
  • Temporal model
  • Alert engine
  • Staff dashboard

Input footage is AI-generated; the detection is real.
